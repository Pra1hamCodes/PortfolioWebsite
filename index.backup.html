"I don't just work with data — I listen to what it's trying to say, then build something that makes others hear it too."

Hi, I'm Prathmesh Pandey — a 22-year-old Computer Engineering student from Mumbai, currently in my final year at VCET. My journey into tech started not with a textbook but with curiosity: why does data look the way it does, and what decisions hide inside it?


That curiosity led me from Android development workshops to full-stack web projects, from robotics competitions to publishing an IEEE research paper on AI-driven misinformation detection — all before graduating. Every project taught me that the best engineers are equally comfortable writing a SQL query and explaining its meaning to someone who has never coded a day in their life.

Outside of code, I lead the VCET Students' Council social media team and build outreach campaigns for the Microsoft Student Learning Club — which taught me that communication and storytelling are just as powerful as any technical skill. Driving 35% audience growth or a 60% jump in engagement isn't just a metric; it's proof that the right message, delivered clearly, moves people.


I believe great analysts are also great communicators. My goal is to land a role where I can blend both — building dashboards, models, and narratives that help organisations make faster, smarter decisions. If that sounds like someone you want on your team, let's talk.
